VaultCap — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 9, 2026

VaultCap is an offline-first Progressive Web App. Your vault data stays on your device unless you explicitly export it. There are no accounts — you unlock with a PIN you choose locally.

What we collect

  • Nothing by default. No accounts, analytics SDKs, or third-party trackers ship in this app.
  • Smart Import (optional): Only when you enable LLM parsing in Settings, text you paste for parsing may be sent to your configured endpoint. See Smart Import below.

Storage & deleting your data

  • App state is stored in localStorage and/or IndexedDB in your browser.
  • Delete vault: Settings → Security → Delete Vault removes all local data and resets the app.
  • Clear site data: In your browser, clear storage for this site to remove all copies without opening the app.
  • Uninstalling the PWA or clearing site data removes local copies permanently.
  • Encrypted .vos backups are files you control — we never receive them.

Network

  • Live FX / gold rates: Public rate APIs with 6-hour cache; no personal data sent.
  • Smart Import / LLM: Off by default. See below.
  • GitHub Pages serves static files only — no server-side access to your vault.

Smart Import (Smart Assistant)

VaultCap offers three parsing paths for Smart Add:

1. Smart Parser (default, offline) — Rule-based parsing on-device. No network. No data leaves your browser.

2. Bundled LLM (opt-in) — Disabled by default in js/config/llm-bundled.js. If you enable it in Settings → Import, requests may go to the configured Cloudflare Worker proxy (POST /parse). Only the text you submit for parsing is transmitted — never your full vault, PIN, or encryption keys.

3. Custom key (optional) — Paste your own API key or proxy URL in Settings → Import. Stored in localStorage on your device only.

If the proxy is unavailable or disabled, Smart Parser is used automatically.

Important: Do not paste full account numbers or passwords into Smart Add. Treat pasted text as non-secret.

Children

  • VaultCap is a general-purpose adult life OS. Parents should supervise device sharing and exports on shared family devices.
