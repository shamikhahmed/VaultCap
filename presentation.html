The Problem

What existing apps get wrong

  • ☁️
    Your data lives on their servers. Every finance app syncs to the cloud — which means a breach, a subpoena, or a shutdown wipes you out.
  • 🔓
    Zero encryption at rest. Most apps store your account numbers, card details, and passwords in plaintext or with reversible encoding.
  • 🌍
    No PK / UK / UAE support. Western-centric apps don't know HBL, Meezan, Sadapay, Jazz, Etisalat, or PSX stocks exist.
  • 🧩
    Fragmented tools. One app for banking, another for passwords, another for documents — no unified view of your financial identity.
  • 💳
    Subscription paywalls. Premium features locked behind recurring fees for data that is yours.
