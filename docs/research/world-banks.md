# World bank & banking-app catalog

Generated by `scripts/build-world-banks.mjs` from `data/world-banks/*.txt`. Machine-readable output: `data/world-banks.json`.

**2336 institutions across 205 countries/territories** — every ISO region in VaultCap's `COUNTRIES` list.

## How this was researched

- Starting point for every country: the central bank or banking regulator's licensed-institution register (named per country below).
- Web research for hard or fast-changing markets (Pacific islands, CEMAC/WAEMU, Eastern Caribbean, conflict-affected states, Gulf digital-bank licences, Nauru's 2025 bank handover). Those source links are listed per country.
- Entries marked **unverified** could not be confirmed against a primary source during this pass (ownership changes, renames, small-market app availability). Treat them as leads.
- `App` = official banking app availability: iOS · Android, Android only (e.g. sanctions-affected markets where apps were pulled from Apple's store), Web only, — (none), ? (not checked).
- SWIFT/BIC is filled only for codes that are widely published (head-office 8-character codes). Blank means not recorded, not "none".
- Logos: `logo` in the JSON points to a bundled file in `assets/banks/` when one exists. Everything else falls back to the app's initials tile. No third-party logo service is called. Logos are trademarks of their owners, used only to identify the institution.
- Banking markets change. Re-check against the regulator before relying on a single entry.
